Assignment 1: Opinion NetworkFormation


The dataset from our recent survey collecting opinions on Technology, Education, Ethics,
and Environment is available at Survey_Results_UC

Your task as a team is to use this dataset to construct and analyze a network based onthese responses and finally submit a report.

General Guidelines
This is an open-ended assignment, giving you the freedom to choose your approach toforming and analyzing the network.
The use of AI tools is permitted.

You must clearly detail the contribution of each team member, as the assignment will begraded individually.

Report Requirements
Your final submission must be a report PDF format of approximately 8 pages. It must include the following sections in this order:
Team Name: The name of your group.
GitHub link to your code: Ensure the repository is accessible and well-organized.
Dataset Documentation: Explain how you interpreted and prepared the survey data.
Pipeline Followed: Describe the network and the step-by-step process you used toconstruct this network from the data. 
Analysis and Visualizations: Provide graphical representations of your network andout line the key metrics.
Results and Discussion: Explain your findings and what the network reveals about theclass opinions.
Individual Contribution: A clear breakdown of the specific tasks completed by eachgroup member.
